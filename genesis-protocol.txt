/*
====================================================================
 STELLAR DOMINION - developer notes
====================================================================
 Currencies:
   stardust  main currency, buys every structure (the "cookie")
   ore       mined; gates bigger structures
   energy    powers advanced structures for big bonuses (no hard stall)
   research  earned from labs; spent only on Technologies
 Building tags:
   core      stardust producers          (boosted by stardust techs)
   mining    ore producers
   power     energy producers
   science   research producers
 Reveal model: each structure unlocks the moment you own the previous
 one, so the store always shows the player's next goal.
 Balance knobs flagged with TODO.
====================================================================
*/
